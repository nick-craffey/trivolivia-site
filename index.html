Internet Origins · Medium

Which object did Cambridge researchers monitor with the first webcam?

  1. A coffee pot
  2. A server cabinet
  3. A laboratory door
  4. A rooftop weather vane
Show the answer and sources

Answer: A coffee pot. Researchers watched the Trojan Room coffee pot from their desks to avoid an unnecessary trip when it was empty. The feed was later made available on the internet.

Sources: University of Cambridge Computer Laboratory and Guinness World Records.
