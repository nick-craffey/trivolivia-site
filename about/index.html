About trivolivia

trivolivia is a free trivia game built around short rounds, curious questions, Olivia’s personality, and a clear trail back to the facts.

Play in your browser View the iPhone app

Official identity: trivolivia is the game at trivolivia.com and Apple App Store app 6747456271. The browser game is free and needs no account for core play.

Daily connects curiosity to the news

Each verified Daily edition has five multiple-choice questions, each connected to a different current story. The question comes first; the explanation, news connection, and sources appear after the answer. When no edition passes review, the game says Daily is being prepared.

Eight topics to explore

Choose Animal Biology, Space and Astronomy, Film Production, Historical Context, Word Origins, Food Origins and Science, Everyday Object Design, or Skateboarding. Each short round shows explanations and sources. Learn how to play.

Simple, local play

Core progress stays on your device. The browser game has no ads or signup requirement. The iPhone app may show older modes until its next update. The Privacy Policy explains data handling.
