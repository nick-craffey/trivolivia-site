About trivolivia

trivolivia is a free, local-first trivia game for people who want more than a score. Every answer comes with a short explanation and supporting sources, while optional challenges and small groups turn useful knowledge into something worth sharing.

Play in your browser View the official iPhone app

Official identity: the product name is trivolivia—one word, styled lowercase. Its canonical website is trivolivia.com, and its iPhone app is listed in Apple’s App Store under the same name.

Trivia with a trail back to the facts

Each round contains five multiple-choice questions. After every answer, trivolivia explains the result and links to two supporting sources. The goal is to make checking a fact and following a new curiosity part of the game instead of an afterthought.

The built-in library contains 200 evergreen questions across 20 topics, including space, animal biology, art, books, history, geography, technology, mathematics, and game design. Browse every trivia topic and try sample questions.

Several ways to play

Daily Five uses five source-backed questions connected to verified current headlines. Week in 20 gathers four recent Daily editions into one resumable weekly round. Hidden Thread asks five apparently unrelated questions before revealing the connection among them.

Players can also choose a subject from Topic Sprint’s reviewed general-audience catalog, browse the built-in Library, continue through an Endless Mix, follow an Adaptive Trail, or return to missed questions in Memory Review. Topic Sprint rejects unsupported text on the device and screens Wikimedia source material before building a round. Most rounds are intentionally short: finish five questions, review what you learned, and stop—or follow a related topic when something catches your attention. The how-to-play guide explains every mode.

A knowledge profile that stays yours

Knowledge DNA is calculated from answers retained on your device. It begins after 20 unique questions and shows evidence across five broad knowledge worlds, with sample sizes and confidence-building thresholds instead of pretending a handful of answers defines you. “Before It Was News” can point out a knowledge world you explored before it appeared in the Daily Five; it is a retrospective connection, not a prediction.

Social when you choose it

No account is required for core play. If you opt in, a pseudonymous social identity unlocks spoiler-safe challenge links, private Braintrust groups of up to 12 people, category-based channels, and “Read the Room” answer distributions. A distribution appears only after you submit that exact edition and at least 10 verified pseudonymous submissions exist; percentages are rounded to five-point buckets. A Braintrust builds shared streaks, weekly standings, category crowns, and a factual Olivia recap from the group’s real results.

trivolivia does not add advertising, behavioral analytics, or tracking pixels. Complete learning history, Knowledge DNA, interests, and review schedules stay local; only the limited Daily and profile data needed for social features goes to the social service.
