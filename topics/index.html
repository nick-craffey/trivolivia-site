A five-question sample round

Pick an answer before opening each explanation. These are real trivolivia Library questions, including the sources shown after an answer in the game.

1 · Space and Astronomy

Which color appears closest to the Sun during a Martian sunset?

  1. Orange
  2. White
  3. Blue
  4. Green
Show the answer and sources

Answer: Blue. A blue glow appears around the Sun during a Martian sunset.

Fine Martian dust scatters light differently from Earth’s air, letting blue light stay concentrated near the setting Sun while the wider sky looks reddish.

Sources: NASA Space Place and Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum.

2 · Animal Biology

How many hearts does an octopus have?

  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. Four
Show the answer and sources

Answer: Three. An octopus has three hearts.

Two hearts send blood through the gills, while a third pumps it around the body. Its copper-based hemocyanin makes that blood appear blue.

Sources: Smithsonian Ocean and NOAA Ocean Service.

3 · Word Origins

The word robot came through Czech from robota, which is closest in meaning to what?

  1. A clockwork toy
  2. A clever servant
  3. Forced labor
  4. A metal machine
Show the answer and sources

Answer: Forced labor. Robot derives from a word for forced labor.

Karel Čapek’s 1920 play popularized robot for artificial workers. The term came from Czech robota, meaning forced labor or drudgery.

Sources: Merriam-Webster and Science Museum.

4 · Accidental Inventions

How did the adhesive created during development of Post-it Notes behave?

  1. Harden only underwater
  2. Conduct electricity
  3. Stick lightly and peel away
  4. Glow under ultraviolet light
Show the answer and sources

Answer: Stick lightly and peel away. The adhesive could be removed and reused.

3M chemist Spencer Silver created small adhesive spheres that gripped surfaces without forming a permanent bond. Art Fry later used the adhesive to keep bookmarks in place in his hymnal.

Sources: American Chemical Society and Lemelson-MIT Program.

5 · Mathematical Patterns

When two fair six-sided dice are rolled, which total can be produced by the most ordered outcomes?

  1. 5
  2. 6
  3. 7
  4. 8
Show the answer and sources

Answer: 7. Seven has six of the 36 possible ordered outcomes.

The pairs 1+6 through 6+1 all total 7, giving six of the 36 equally likely outcomes. No other total has as many routes.

Sources: Portland Community College and University of California, Berkeley.

How the questions are checked

Each built-in card is reviewed for a clear answer, a concise explanation, and two distinct HTTPS sources that support the claim. The Library focuses on evergreen subjects; date-sensitive trivia is handled separately in Today’s News.
