10 geographic perspectives questions

1 · Warm-up

In which direction does the Detroit–Windsor tunnel travel from Detroit into Canada?

  1. North
  2. West
  3. South
  4. East
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: South. The route from Detroit into Canada travels south.

The Detroit River bends so that Windsor, Ontario, lies mainly south of central Detroit. Although Canada is generally north of the United States, this section of the border runs differently.

Sources: City of Windsor and Tourism Windsor Essex Pelee Island.

2 · Warm-up

Crossing the 180th meridian makes Alaska both the westernmost U.S. state and what else?

  1. Closest to Europe
  2. Southernmost
  3. Most landlocked
  4. Easternmost
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Easternmost. Alaska is both the westernmost and easternmost U.S. state.

Some Aleutian islands lie west of the 180th meridian in eastern longitudes. By longitude, Alaska reaches farther east and west than any other U.S. state.

Sources: U.S. Geological Survey and State of Alaska.

3 · Core

Which continent is crossed by both the Equator and Prime Meridian, placing land in all four hemispheres?

  1. Africa
  2. Asia
  3. Europe
  4. South America
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Africa. Africa has land in all four hemispheres.

The Equator crosses central Africa and the Prime Meridian crosses the continent's west. Together, the lines place African land in all four combinations of hemispheres.

Sources: National Geographic Society and Encyclopaedia Britannica.

4 · Core

When a house straddles Belgium and the Netherlands, what usually determines its official country?

  1. Where the kitchen sits
  2. Where the front door stands
  3. Which side has more floor space
  4. Which country issued the first deed
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Where the front door stands. The location of the main entrance determines the address.

Baarle's Belgian and Dutch enclaves cross streets and buildings. Under the front-door rule, a straddling property's official address follows the country containing its main entrance.

Sources: Municipality of Baarle-Hertog and Omroep Brabant.

5 · Core

Which summit is farthest from Earth's center, although Everest is higher above sea level?

  1. Denali
  2. Kilimanjaro
  3. Chimborazo
  4. Aconcagua
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Chimborazo. Chimborazo is farther from Earth's center than Everest.

Ecuador's Chimborazo stands near the Equator, where Earth bulges outward. Its summit is therefore about 2.1 kilometers farther from Earth's center than Everest's.

Sources: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration and NASA Earth Observatory.

6 · Core

By actual land area, Africa is approximately how many times the size of Greenland?

  1. 2
  2. 5
  3. 9
  4. 14
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: 14. Africa is about fourteen times the size of Greenland.

Africa covers about 30 million square kilometers, compared with Greenland's roughly 2.2 million. The Mercator projection greatly enlarges high latitudes, making Greenland appear much larger than it is.

Sources: National Geographic Society and U.S. Government Publishing Office.

7 · Core

Which country surrounds Lesotho?

  1. South Africa
  2. Eswatini
  3. Botswana
  4. Mozambique
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: South Africa. Lesotho is completely surrounded by South Africa.

Lesotho is an enclave: its entire border is shared with South Africa. It is one of only three sovereign countries completely surrounded by a single other country.

Sources: The Commonwealth and Lesotho Ministry of Home Affairs.

8 · Core

Relative to the Panama Canal's Atlantic entrance, where is its Pacific entrance?

  1. Due north
  2. Farther east
  3. Farther west
  4. At the same longitude
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Farther east. The Pacific entrance lies east of the Atlantic entrance.

Panama curves in a broad S shape, and the canal runs from northwest to southeast. Ships traveling from the Atlantic toward the Pacific therefore finish farther east.

Sources: Library of Congress and NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

9 · Challenge

If it is 9 a.m. Monday on Little Diomede, approximately what time is it on Big Diomede?

  1. 6 a.m. Monday
  2. Noon Monday
  3. 6 a.m. Tuesday
  4. Noon Tuesday
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: 6 a.m. Tuesday. The islands' official times differ by about 21 hours.

Russia's Big Diomede and Alaska's Little Diomede sit on opposite sides of the International Date Line. Their official time zones place them about 21 hours apart.

Sources: U.S. Geological Survey and NASA Science.

10 · Challenge

Through French Guiana, which country shares France's longest land border?

  1. Argentina
  2. Colombia
  3. Suriname
  4. Brazil
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Brazil. France's longest land border is with Brazil.

French Guiana is legally part of France, so its roughly 730-kilometer boundary with Brazil is a French border. It is longer than any border in metropolitan France.

Sources: Sénat and France Diplomatie.
