10 everyday object design questions

1 · Warm-up

The small pocket inside many jeans was originally designed to hold which item?

  1. A pocket watch
  2. A train ticket
  3. Loose gold coins
  4. A matchbox
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: A pocket watch. The small pocket was designed for a pocket watch.

Jeans made in the late 1800s included a fitted pocket for the pocket watches commonly carried at the time. The feature remained after wristwatches became more common.

Sources: Levi Strauss & Co. and The Indian Express.

2 · Warm-up

Which materials make up most of the writing core in an ordinary wooden pencil?

  1. Lead and tin
  2. Graphite and clay
  3. Charcoal and wax
  4. Carbon-black ink
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Graphite and clay. Pencil cores are made primarily from graphite and clay.

Graphite produces the mark, while clay controls hardness and strength. The term “lead” reflects an early misidentification; metallic lead is not part of the core.

Sources: U.S. Geological Survey and American Chemical Society.

3 · Core

What do the raised marks on the F and J keys help a typist locate without looking?

  1. Speed up key repeat
  2. Separate vowels from consonants
  3. Locate the home row by touch
  4. Mark the most-used letters
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Locate the home row by touch. The marks identify the home-row position by touch.

The raised marks let the index fingers find F and J without visual guidance. The remaining fingers can then take their standard positions on the home row.

Sources: Microsoft and University of Colorado Boulder.

4 · Core

Why do many ballpoint pen caps have a hole through the top?

  1. Keep ink from drying
  2. Stop the cap cracking
  3. Balance writing pressure
  4. Let air pass if swallowed
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Let air pass if swallowed. The opening can maintain airflow if the cap is inhaled.

Safety standards reduce asphyxiation risk by requiring certain small caps to be ventilated or too large to swallow. A ventilated cap can preserve some airflow if it is inhaled.

Sources: International Organization for Standardization and Google Patents.

5 · Core

A raised wear bar is flush with the surrounding tire tread. What does this indicate?

  1. Replace the tire soon
  2. Add more air
  3. Rotate the tire
  4. Check the wheel alignment
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Replace the tire soon. A flush wear bar indicates that the tread has reached its limit.

Treadwear bars sit at 2/32 inch. When the surrounding tread wears down to their height, the tire is worn out and should be replaced.

Sources: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration and Michelin.

6 · Core

The stiff brushes along an escalator's sides mainly help riders do what?

  1. Sweep debris from the steps
  2. Keep feet away from the moving skirt
  3. Discharge static electricity
  4. Detect oversized luggage
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Keep feet away from the moving skirt. The brushes guide feet away from the gap beside the steps.

The brushes guide feet and loose clothing away from the narrow gap beside the moving steps. They help prevent entrapment rather than clean footwear.

Sources: Otis Worldwide and California Department of Industrial Relations.

7 · Core

How does the metal mesh in a microwave door permit a view of the food while containing microwaves?

  1. It traps steam inside
  2. It cools the door glass
  3. Its holes pass light but block microwaves
  4. It cancels waves with electricity
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Its holes pass light but block microwaves. The openings pass visible light but block microwaves.

Visible-light wavelengths pass through the small openings. Much longer microwave wavelengths are reflected by the conductive screen.

Sources: MIT School of Engineering and GE Appliances.

8 · Core

What does the arrow beside the fuel-pump icon on many dashboards indicate?

  1. The required fuel grade
  2. How full the tank is
  3. The nearest pump direction
  4. Which side has the filler door
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Which side has the filler door. The arrow points to the side with the fuel-filler door.

The arrow points toward the vehicle's fuel-filler door. It provides a quick reference when driving an unfamiliar vehicle.

Sources: Chevrolet and Nissan.

9 · Challenge

Why is the metal hook at the end of a tape measure designed to slide slightly?

  1. Compensate for the hook's thickness
  2. Lock the tape when tilted
  3. Absorb drops and impacts
  4. Reset at every whole foot
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Compensate for the hook's thickness. The hook's movement compensates for its thickness.

For an outside measurement, the hook moves outward; for an inside measurement, it moves inward. The travel equals the hook's thickness, keeping both readings accurate.

Sources: Stanley Black & Decker and Google Patents.

10 · Challenge

Why were ridges added to the edges of precious-metal coins?

  1. Help coins stack evenly
  2. Reveal if metal was clipped away
  3. Let merchants count by touch
  4. Make a clearer ring when dropped
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Reveal if metal was clipped away. Reeded edges revealed when metal had been removed.

People once clipped valuable silver or gold from smooth coin edges. Milled or reeded patterns made missing metal visible and helped deter the practice.

Sources: Federal Reserve Bank of New York and Numismatic Guaranty Company.
