10 food origins and science questions

1 · Warm-up

Peanuts belong to the same plant family as which familiar food group?

  1. Peas and beans
  2. Walnuts and pecans
  3. Sesame and sunflower
  4. Coconut and palm
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Peas and beans. Peanuts are legumes rather than tree nuts.

Peanuts belong to the legume family alongside peas and beans. After pollination, their flower stalks bend downward and place the developing pods in the soil.

Sources: U.S. Department of Agriculture and National Park Service.

2 · Warm-up

The vanilla pod is the cured fruit of which kind of plant?

  1. A tropical grass
  2. An orchid
  3. A climbing palm
  4. A flowering shrub
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: An orchid. Vanilla pods are the fruits of orchids.

Vanilla comes from climbing orchids in the genus Vanilla. Their long fruits are harvested, cured, and darkened into the aromatic pods commonly called beans.

Sources: University of California, Davis Botanical Conservatory and Smithsonian Gardens.

3 · Core

In which pale inner structure of a chile pepper is capsaicin concentrated?

  1. The outer skin
  2. The hollow center
  3. The ribs and placenta
  4. The green stem
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: The ribs and placenta. Capsaicin is concentrated in the placenta and inner ribs.

Capsaicin glands are located mainly in the placenta, the pale tissue and ribs that hold the seeds. Seeds can acquire capsaicin through contact, but do not produce it.

Sources: American Chemical Society and New Mexico State University.

4 · Core

White chocolate contains cocoa butter but lacks which component found in dark and milk chocolate?

  1. Milk fat
  2. Sugar
  3. Cocoa butter
  4. Cocoa solids
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Cocoa solids. White chocolate contains no nonfat cocoa solids.

Cocoa butter supplies the fat, while the darker nonfat cocoa solids are absent. This composition accounts for white chocolate's color and milder flavor.

Sources: U.S. Food and Drug Administration and Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.

5 · Core

Caesar salad is credited to a restaurant in which city?

  1. Tijuana
  2. Rome
  3. New York City
  4. Paris
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Tijuana. Caesar salad is credited to a restaurant in Tijuana.

The salad is credited to Caesar Cardini's restaurant in Tijuana, Mexico, in the 1920s. Its name refers to the restaurateur, not to a Roman ruler.

Sources: Caesar's Restaurant-Bar Tijuana and Smithsonian Magazine.

6 · Core

Which combination allows popcorn to expand rather than simply roast?

  1. Dry starch beneath a porous shell
  2. Moisture trapped by a tough hull
  3. Oil stored in a flexible skin
  4. Air pockets inside a brittle core
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Moisture trapped by a tough hull. A hard hull traps moisture until pressure ruptures the kernel.

A popcorn kernel's tough hull traps moisture as heat converts it to vapor. Pressure rises until the hull ruptures, allowing the softened starch to expand and turn outward.

Sources: USDA National Agricultural Library and Purdue University College of Agriculture.

7 · Core

What is the part of a coffee cherry that is later roasted and called a bean?

  1. The dried pulp
  2. The fruit's lining
  3. A seed
  4. A tiny stem
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: A seed. Coffee beans are the seeds of coffee cherries.

Coffee plants produce fruits commonly called cherries, usually with two seeds inside. Processing removes the skin and pulp before those seeds are dried and roasted.

Sources: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and University of California, Davis Library.

8 · Core

Why does gelatin usually set with canned pineapple but not with fresh pineapple?

  1. Heat neutralizes its gel-blocking acid
  2. Heat removes its water-binding sugars
  3. Heat softens its gelatin-trapping fibers
  4. Heat disables its protein-cutting enzyme
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Heat disables its protein-cutting enzyme. Canning heat denatures the enzyme bromelain.

Fresh pineapple contains bromelain, a protease that breaks down gelatin's protein chains before they form a firm structure. Canning heat denatures the enzyme, allowing the gelatin to set.

Sources: American Chemical Society and Scientific American.

9 · Challenge

Which central ingredient appeared in the Asian sauces associated with ketchup's origin?

  1. Fish
  2. Tomatoes
  3. Berries
  4. Chile peppers
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Fish. Ketchup's ancestry includes fermented fish sauce.

Early Asian sauces linked to ketchup were salty mixtures of fermented fish. British adaptations used ingredients such as mushrooms and walnuts before tomato-based versions became standard in the United States.

Sources: Smithsonian Magazine and University of Illinois Extension.

10 · Challenge

Nutmeg is the seed; which part of the fruit becomes mace?

  1. The dried fruit pulp
  2. The lacy seed covering
  3. The unopened flower
  4. The inner tree bark
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: The lacy seed covering. Mace is the red aril surrounding the nutmeg seed.

Nutmeg is the seed, while mace is the red, netlike aril around it. The two structures are separated and dried to produce distinct spices.

Sources: Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew and Missouri Botanical Garden.
