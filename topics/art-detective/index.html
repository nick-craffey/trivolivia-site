10 art details questions

1 · Warm-up

Which distant landmark echoes the shape of the wave in The Great Wave?

  1. A full moon
  2. A fishing mast
  3. Mount Fuji
  4. A shrine gate
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Mount Fuji. Mount Fuji echoes the wave's contour.

Hokusai places the large foreground wave against a small, steady Mount Fuji. The white crest frames the distant peak and repeats its shape.

Sources: The Metropolitan Museum of Art and British Museum.

2 · Warm-up

Who posed as the farmer and daughter in American Gothic?

  1. A farmer and his wife
  2. Two touring actors
  3. Wood and his mother
  4. His dentist and his sister
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: His dentist and his sister. Grant Wood's dentist and sister posed for the pair.

Wood recruited his dentist, Dr. B. H. McKeeby, and his sister Nan. He intended the figures to represent a farmer and his daughter, rather than a married couple.

Sources: American Gothic House Center and Smithsonian Magazine.

3 · Core

Museums classify Girl with a Pearl Earring as which kind of work?

  1. A character study
  2. A royal portrait
  3. A self-portrait
  4. A wedding portrait
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: A character study. The painting is a tronie, or character study.

A tronie is a study of a face, expression, costume, or character rather than a commissioned portrait of a known sitter. The model's identity is not documented.

Sources: Mauritshuis and The Frick Collection.

4 · Core

Which object is closest in size to the original Persistence of Memory?

  1. A postage stamp
  2. A laptop screen
  3. A movie poster
  4. A dining-table top
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: A laptop screen. The canvas is approximately the size of a laptop screen.

The painting measures about 9.5 by 13 inches. Large reproductions can obscure the modest scale of the physical work.

Sources: Museum of Modern Art and Fundació Gala-Salvador Dalí.

5 · Core

Whom does the mirror at the back of Las Meninas reveal?

  1. The artist's parents
  2. The queen's attendants
  3. The king and queen
  4. The painter and his wife
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: The king and queen. The mirror reflects King Philip IV and Queen Mariana.

The rear mirror shows King Philip IV and Queen Mariana. Their position creates ambiguity about whether the royal pair occupy the viewer's position.

Sources: Museo Nacional del Prado and Smarthistory.

6 · Core

Which message does Van Eyck's inscription in the Arnolfini Portrait convey?

  1. Van Eyck loved them
  2. Van Eyck remembers
  3. Van Eyck saw everything
  4. Jan van Eyck was here
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Jan van Eyck was here. The inscription states, “Jan van Eyck was here, 1434.”

The Latin inscription above the mirror translates as “Jan van Eyck was here, 1434.” Its prominent placement presents the painter as both artist and witness.

Sources: The National Gallery and Dallas Museum of Art.

7 · Core

Images of the bombing of Guernica reached Picasso through which medium?

  1. Newspapers
  2. Stained-glass windows
  3. Military uniforms
  4. Newsreel film
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Newspapers. The palette recalls newspaper photography.

Picasso learned of Guernica's destruction through newspaper coverage. Scholars connect the mural's stark monochrome to the press photographs that documented the bombing.

Sources: Museum of Modern Art and Smarthistory.

8 · Core

Why did a daytime scene become known as The Night Watch?

  1. A later copy changed its setting
  2. Dirt and varnish darkened it
  3. Curators displayed it by candlelight
  4. The militia met only after sunset
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Dirt and varnish darkened it. Dirt and darkened varnish made the daytime scene appear nocturnal.

Layers of dirt and darkened varnish made the daytime scene look nocturnal, which helped the later title persist. Cleaning revealed a militia company moving into daylight.

Sources: City of Amsterdam and Smarthistory.

9 · Challenge

For which part of Florence Cathedral was David first commissioned?

  1. Inside a side chapel
  2. Above the main doorway
  3. High on an exterior buttress
  4. Beside the bell tower
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: High on an exterior buttress. David was intended for a high exterior buttress.

The figure was commissioned for a buttress high on Florence Cathedral. After its completion, the sculpture's weight and perceived quality led officials to select a ground-level site instead.

Sources: Opera di Santa Maria del Fiore and Victoria and Albert Museum.

10 · Challenge

Before stealing the Mona Lisa in 1911, Vincenzo Peruggia had worked at the Louvre in which trade?

  1. Night guard
  2. Frame maker
  3. Curator's assistant
  4. Glazier
Show the answer, explanation, and sources

Answer: Glazier. Peruggia had worked at the Louvre as a glazier.

Peruggia knew the Louvre's routines from his work as a glazier. He removed the painting, concealed it beneath his work clothes, and kept it for more than two years.

Sources: Musée du Louvre and Library of Congress.
