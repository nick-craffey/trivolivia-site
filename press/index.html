Brand identity: trivolivia is one word, styled lowercase. The canonical website is trivolivia.com, and the official App Store listing is Apple ID 6747456271.

Product at a glance

  • Format: free five-question trivia rounds with an explanation after every answer.
  • Sources: two supporting links on each evergreen Library card.
  • Library: 200 evergreen questions across 20 themed topics.
  • Ways to play: Daily News, Topic Sprint, Library, Endless Mix, Adaptive Trail, and Memory Review.
  • Access: playable in a modern web browser and available for iPhone.
  • Privacy: no account required; trivolivia adds no ads, analytics, or tracking pixels.

Short description

trivolivia is a free trivia game built around five-question rounds, concise explanations, and supporting sources. It is available on the web and for iPhone, with no account required and no ads or analytics added by trivolivia.

What makes it different

The result of a question is not the end of the round. trivolivia shows a useful explanation immediately, keeps the supporting sources close, and lets a player follow related topics through an adaptive learning trail. Short rounds make it easy to stop after five or keep going when a fact opens a new line of curiosity.

The public editorial method and corrections policy explains how evergreen and date-sensitive questions are sourced, validated, and corrected.
